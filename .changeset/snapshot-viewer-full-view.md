A snapshot opens on the whole window instead of a side panel: one slim bar (Back, title, original URL, the views, full screen, open in a new tab) over the archived page itself, which is the first view (**Page**, formerly **DOM**), or the video when there is one (**Media**). **Details** (status, tags, extractors, history, Delete) is a view like the others (`I`); `F` shows the page full screen and `Escape` goes back to the list page you came from. The archived page follows the app's theme when its CSS has a dark mode. The snapshot list opens as **Cards** with large screenshots; rows are still one click away. New shortcuts: `/` focuses the search or filter field of the page, `Ctrl+B` (`Cmd+B`) shows or hides the sidebar. **Raw** is now **Files**.
